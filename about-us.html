WHO WE ARE

TATAgency Limited (The Acclivial Travellers Escapade Limited) is a registered company with the Corporate Affairs Commission both locally and internationally accredited & licensed by travel & tourism governing bodies to operate haven met all the necessary requirements and standards. We have been a travel solution to over 3000 clients both corporately and individually since its inception in September 2018. With our head office in Yorkton Canada & our branch in Lagos Nigeria we have over the years brought the best of travel services in Visa application & advisory service, vacation/holidays, flight booking & ticketing, travel insurance, transfers and lot more.

We are a team with experience, expertise and we all are corporately committed to the success & satisfaction of of all our clients. Over the past 2years, the company has risen to the level of one of the best emerging travel agency in Nigeria competing with the best in the industry which is due to the dedication & motivation of our management and staff. We are here to raise the standard of travel industry through our infallible services and core competences.
